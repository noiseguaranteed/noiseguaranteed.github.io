Belfast · a noise-positive organisation

Instruments you can open, fix and change.

We're a Belfast collective that builds electronic instruments you can open, repair and make your own. If you're a disabled musician, we'll design one with you for free. Our workshops are open to everyone, and their fees cover the cost.

01

why this matters

Instruments sold as accessible are often expensive, designed without disabled musicians in the room, and hard to repair. When one breaks, or stops fitting the way you play, it ends up in a cupboard.

So we design instruments with disabled musicians, from cheap, standard parts. You decide how yours works, and you learn to fix and change it yourself. An instrument you can maintain keeps getting played.

03

how it fits together

workshops

  1. Open to everyone, disabled or not. You build an instrument and learn how it works.

  2. Each fee covers the kit and the room. The rest pays for instruments.

instruments

  1. If you're a disabled musician, we can design an instrument with you, around how you play. It's free.

  2. It's built on the same open parts, so you, and everyone from the workshops, can fix and change it.

04

critical making

We learn about technology by building it, and ask questions as we go. Where did this chip come from? Who was it designed for? Who gets left out?

Between us we've designed commercial synths, turned drums into controllers and wired up installations, but we still start with naive questions. Anyone can ask them, and they get sharper as you build.
