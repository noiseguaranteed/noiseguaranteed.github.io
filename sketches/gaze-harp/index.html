gaze harp

Play pentatonic scales with your eyes. Inspired by the EyeHarp.

  1. 1melody: look at a note's square to play it. Look at the centre to rest. The inner ring is low, the outer ring is high.
  2. 2loop editor: look at a grid square to switch it on or off. The loop keeps playing under the melody.
  3. 3strings: sweep your eyes across the strings to strum them. Looking higher up makes a brighter sound.

The buttons along the top need a longer look. Gaze can come from anything that moves the mouse pointer with your eyes, or from a WebSocket stream. Keys: 1 2 3 layers, [ ] melody gaze time, Space loop, D drone, Esc settings, F11 full screen. noise guaranteed home
