Three people, plenty of noise.

who we are

Alex Lucas

A product designer turned researcher in accessible music technology. Alex designed the Novation Peak and Circuit Mono Station, then did a PhD at SARC, Queen's University Belfast, co-designing instruments with disabled musicians. He leads the instrument programme.

Barry Cullen

An audio and video enthusiast working with DIY electronics, moving images, interactive installation, DJing and VJing. His performances mix improvisation, folk idioms and noise.

Saul Rayson

A composer, improviser and percussionist who trained in the UK and at The Drummers Collective in New York. Saul extends and adapts drums, using them as both sound source and controller.
