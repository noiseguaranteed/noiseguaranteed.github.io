Upcoming Workshops

Shoestring Synthesis

In this four-hour workshop we'll build a cheap and dirty oscillator based on the infamous 40106 integrated circuit. We'll craft some handmade wonky sensors for controlling your oscillator and explore some basic sound modulation techniques. No prior experience in electronics or music is required.

  • Location: Cityside Mall, Belfast
  • Cost: free
