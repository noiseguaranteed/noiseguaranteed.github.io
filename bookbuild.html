We meet on a semi-regular basis at SARC to talk through technical documentation related to handmade electronic music. This is similar to a book club, however, we also build the circuits described in the documents we read. We strive to make this group inclusive of all abilities. If you're interested in joining then please get in touch. We're currently working through the documentation for EDU range of synthesiser modules from Erica Synths.
